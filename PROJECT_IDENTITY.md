The **Duet** name, its project artwork, and the presentation of an "official Duet build" identify releases created and maintained by Lauren Landau as the project's sole canonical maintainer. The software license does not grant permission to use those identifiers in a way that suggests an unofficial build is maintained, approved, or supported by the Duet project.

## You May

- State accurately that a project is based on or compatible with Duet.
- Link to the official Duet repository.
- Use unmodified official screenshots when discussing or reviewing Duet.
- Fork the source and publish modified builds under a clearly distinct name.

## Please Do Not

- Call a modified build an official Duet release.
- Use the Duet name or artwork as the primary identity of a fork.
- Remove or obscure the copyright, license, authorship, or provenance record.
- Imply that Lauren Landau or the Duet project endorses an unrelated build, service, product, or repository.
