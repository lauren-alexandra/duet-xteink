# Product

Duet is an independent, one-person open-source project created and maintained by Lauren Landau, with coding tools used under her direction and review.

## Register

product

## Users

Duet is for readers using small, button-driven XTEINK e-paper devices. The primary workflow is finding a book quickly, reading comfortably for long sessions, and preserving progress and reading history across firmware updates.

## Product Purpose

Duet turns the XTEINK into a dependable personal library and reading device. Success means the interface remains legible and predictable on a monochrome e-paper screen, books are easy to browse and resume, and optional library detail never compromises reading stability.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, book-focused, and capable. The interface should feel personal and polished without competing with the books themselves.

## Anti-references

- Decorative interfaces that sacrifice usable space to framing or ornament.
- Cover layouts that make folders look like books or hide the library hierarchy.
- Dense metadata that makes titles hard to scan.
- Motion-dependent interactions that do not translate to e-paper.

## Design Principles

- Keep reading and resuming a book faster than managing the library.
- Preserve familiar button behavior across every view.
- Show richer information progressively, without crowding the primary selection.
- Treat memory, storage, and refresh cost as part of the user experience.
- Keep user data and reading history recoverable across updates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support adjustable fonts and sizes, including OpenDyslexic, strong monochrome contrast, generous selection outlines, readable labels, and interactions that require no animation or color perception. All layouts must remain usable on both X3 and X4 screen dimensions.
